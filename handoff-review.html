1. Her three questions — answered

Which GPU? → the L4 is a good fit ✓

One L4 (24 GB) is enough — the platform uses a single GPU (no multi-card/NVLink). It has ample VRAM for the 14B model (~11 GB needed) and draws ¼ the power of our current A10G (72 W vs 300 W). The one trade-off: the L4's 300 GB/s memory bandwidth is half the A10G's, so per-investigation latency will be somewhat higher than our ~38 s median — fine for triage (180 s budget, not interactive). Minimum viable GPU is any NVIDIA card with ≥ 12 GB VRAM.
